# Reference: How Idle Angels Works

Research snapshot from 2026-10-15, compiled from web search results (guides, store pages, wikis,
reviews). Many guides date from before the game's **December 2023 overhaul** (new UI, redrawn art,
MR tier), so check details in the live game before relying on them. Items marked
*(unverified)* could not be confirmed.

> **Use this as a reference, not a template.** Game mechanics are generally fair to borrow, but
> names, characters, art, text and UI layouts are not. Our versions must be original.

## Basics

- Developer credited as **JoyPort**; originally published by MUJOY, now **Neorigin Games**.
  Launched 2019–2020 (sources conflict). Android + iOS. Rated 17+.
- Re-released under many subtitles ("Realm of Goddess", "Season of Legends", "6th Anniv", …), all
  the same app.
- **Performance:** a long-running *mid-tier* game, not a top grosser. Google Play shows 1M+ installs
  at 4.6★. Sensor Tower estimated about $129K **US** revenue in Q4 2022.
- **Crowded niche:** other angel-themed idle gacha games include Angel Legion, Idle Epic: Angels of
  Fate, and Seraphim Saga. **The theme alone is not a differentiator.**

## Core loop

- **Idle rewards:** EXP, hero EXP, level-cap material and coins per minute. The rate goes up when a
  campaign boss is beaten. Rewards stop when the storage chest is full (cap hours *(unverified)*).
- **Quick-Combat:** instantly claim 2 hours of idle rewards. Once per day free; more with VIP.
- **Campaign:** chapters of 10–20 stages. Waves auto-play; the player taps "Boss Battle" to advance.

## Battle

- **6 angels:** slots 1–3 are the front row (targeted first), slots 4–6 the back row. Slots unlock
  by player level.
- **6 substitutes** (unlocked at level 50) replace fallen angels.
- Fully automatic skills: a charge ring fills, then the skill fires.
- **Counter system:** 3 classes. Warrior beats Archer, Archer beats Mage, Mage beats Warrior, for
  +20% damage. No element/faction wheel was found.
- **"Fates":** bonuses for fielding specific angels together. **"Magic Circle":** a bonus based on
  how many angels survive.

## Characters and gacha

- 100+ angels based on world mythology (Hera, Odin, Ra, Nuwa, …).
- **Rarity ladder:** N → R → R+ → SR → SR+ → SSR → SSR+ → UR → UR+ → MR. Angels climb tiers.
- **Banners:** Normal (50 diamonds per pull, R guaranteed within 10) and Elite (300 per pull, SR
  guaranteed within 30, SR+ within 80). Epic (level 60) and Legend (level 100) banners come later.
  Each banner has one free daily pull. Pity resets on a hit.
- **Shards:** about 50–55 shards create an angel. Duplicates become shards, or "Incarnation Shards"
  once the angel is maxed.
- **Wish Pool:** pick a featured angel and get +2 of its shards per 10 pulls.
- **Relic gacha:** SSR 0.5% (pity 200), SR 4.5% (pity 30), R 45% (pity 10).

## Progression

- **Level** with hero EXP; **level-cap breaks** with a separate material.
- **Awakening (star-up)** costs coins + shards and unlocks skills. Each awakening also gives an
  **account-wide** stat bonus (Angels Temple), so old heroes stay useful.
- **Inheritance:** move a hero's upgrades onto another hero.
- **Gear** (weapon, armor, ring, …) with enhance and smelt. **Relics** that upgrade up to UR+5; top
  relics fit specific angels only.
- **Favorability** (level 30): gifts, 2 interactions per day, and adventures give stats and story
  scenes. "Marriage" needs 2,000 favorability plus a ring bought in the shop.
- **Paid outfits grant stats**, so cosmetics affect power.

## Modes (unlock level)

Guild and Clash of Guilds (12) · Arena (20) · World Boss (25) · Adventure (30) · Sky Tower (40) ·
Cross-server Glory Arena (50) · Secret Abyss, a daily 3×5 dungeon with no HP refill (60) ·
Angel Trial (60) · Star Trial, 19 stages with no healing (70). Daily activity points open reward
chests.

## Monetization

VIP levels (bought with diamonds; add Quick-Combats and other perks), packs from about $1 to
$50, passes, daily-recharge and limited-time offers, stat-granting outfits, a ring for marriage,
and optional rewarded ads ("bonus center"). Monthly card and battle-pass details *(unverified)*.

## What players say

- **Praised:** art and skins, free high-tier characters and gift codes, low time commitment,
  frequent events.
- **Criticized:** pay-to-win and events tuned for whales; crashes, disconnects, rollbacks and lost
  accounts; poor support; mindless auto combat; fan service as the only draw; expensive
  stat-granting cosmetics; free players progress far too slowly (by one estimate, about 600 free
  scrolls in several months vs about 2,700 to max one top angel).
