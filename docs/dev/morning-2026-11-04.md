## A heroine's ultimate is a cut-in (commit 5d3f333)

- **The full cut-in.** Ink bars slam over the screen and it cuts hard to her poster: paper, a straight
  ink band with her red halftone print. The camera climbs from her hips to a cowboy shot as she surges
  up, her head snaps to the lens, and the skill's key word slams up the band's edge.
- **The impact frame.** She coils and lunges, and on the impact frame the whole poster prints as its
  plates (paper to ink, band to signal, her a hard two-tone). An ink crown rendered in Blender bursts
  where the blow lands.
- **Back to the field.** A hard cut returns to the field, punched in on the target, where the same
  crown stamps.
- **When it plays.** Each heroine's first ultimate in a fight and every one you fire by hand get the
  full cut-in; later ones start at her pose. A tap skips to the strike.
- **New setting:** Settings > Battle > Ultimate cut-ins: **Every / First / By hand**. First is the
  default.

## Every hit is printed (commit 841c070)

The other 90% of a fight was still code shapes: glowing bolts, a white crescent, spark rings, twenty
floating numbers at once (some printed over the heroines), a white flash and a random shake. Three
designs were drawn up and judged, the winner built and filmed six times, then three reviewers (look,
bugs, iPhone performance) went through it and it was reworked again.

- **Every blow on a foe prints an ink mark rendered in Blender.** A slash is a dry-brush stroke
  across her and a guardian's thrust is a jab. A shot flies as a drop of ink and lands as a splat. A
  crit crosses two strokes as an X, and assassins cut an X too.
- **The impact.** The struck foe snaps to a two-tone print for a beat and is knocked back, and the
  camera kicks along the blow.
- **Deaths.** A fallen foe holds her two-tone print, then lifts off as paper dots and leaves an ink
  blot on the street. A fallen heroine fades into a quiet ink print and stays on the line.
- **Numbers are calm.** Each foe has one running total beside her health tag. Only your camera
  heroine's crit gets the big red slab, so red is the one accent.
- **The heroines are never covered.** What lands on a heroine shows as a small paper slip on her
  squad card, and nothing is printed on the paintings. Light hits no longer make her flinch or go grey.
- **Sound.** New hit sounds: press, brush swish, flick, splat, tick, peel, a roller for heals and foil
  for shields.
