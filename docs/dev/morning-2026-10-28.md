## Decisions I made without you

- **Halloween:** three bats (two black, one pumpkin: black ones vanish over the poster's black
  type); candlelight from 18:00 to 05:00 replaces the night lamp that evening.
- **18+ skins:** when 18+ is on, a skin shows its own 18+ look only once it is listed in
  `data/adult_skins.json`. The 60 older 18+ skin looks on disk were painted before the skins were
  re-rendered and don't match them, so the list starts empty; the art session adds each new one.
- **Short phones:** the screenshot tool now simulates a home-button iPhone (SE) properly; several
  "broken on short phones" problems were the old simulation, not the game.
- **Stamped days** (holidays, birthdays) are kept about ten years instead of 400 days, so the
  profile's record keeps them.
- **Under the hood:** one colour palette instead of 41 copies; the War goes through one interface,
  so a server can replace the offline simulation without touching any screen.
