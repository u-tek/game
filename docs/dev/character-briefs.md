# Character Briefs (art direction v2)

These briefs **override the `look` field in `data/heroes.json`** for all art work.

## Content direction

The owner wants the game's art to be as alluring as App Store rules allow, in the tradition of
Idle Angels and similar fan-service gacha games. Rules that every piece of art must follow:

- **Adults only.** Every character is an adult with an apparent age of 20 or older: adult body
  proportions, adult facial structure, adult styling. Never combine suggestive content with
  childlike bodies, faces, clothing (school uniforms, etc.) or behavior.
- **Suggestive, not explicit.** Allowed: revealing and form-fitting costumes, cleavage, bare
  shoulders, backs, midriffs and thighs, high slits, garters, stockings, corsetry, sheer layers
  **over opaque coverage**, alluring poses and expressions, and implied nudity in special art
  where hair, wings, cloth, light or composition does the covering. Never: visible nipples,
  genitals or pubic area (including through sheer fabric), sexual acts, or poses that mimic them.
- **Rating target:** App Store 18+ (frequent/intense mature or suggestive themes).
  Google Play's policy is stricter about minimally clothed, sexually suggestive poses. If the game
  goes to Android, plan a "standard" outfit alongside the "alluring" one.
- Allure should come with character: each hero keeps a distinct personality, silhouette and
  role read. Men get the same treatment (open coats, bare chests, toned physiques).

## Celestial (white, gold, sky blue)

- **Seraphiel**, Legendary Caster, appears mid-20s. Statuesque, curvy seraph with six wings
  framing her (two large pairs, one small pair at the head) and a golden sunray halo crown. Very long
  platinum hair that drapes over her body. White-and-gold layered gown: plunging neckline held
  by gold filigree, open back between the wings, sheer outer layers over an opaque inner gown,
  thigh-high slit, gold body chains and anklets. Floating golden tuning-fork sceptre. Serene,
  alluring half-smile, eyes usually closed.
- **Cassiel**, Epic Guardian, appears mid-20s. Athletic, confident paladin. Ornate white-gold
  armor that leaves the midriff and upper thighs bare: sculpted breastplate, big pauldrons,
  gorget, armored gauntlets and thigh-high greaves, sky-blue tabard as a loincloth panel.
  Short silver bob. Huge kite shield with a sunburst. Two feathered wings, simple halo.
- **Liora**, Epic Support, appears early 20s, clearly adult. Soft, curvy healer. Off-shoulder
  cream corset dress with sheer rose sleeves and a short layered skirt, gold embroidery,
  ribbons in rose-gold twin tails. Golden lyre. Two small fluffy wings, flower halo. Warm,
  teasing smile.
- **Uriela**, Rare Striker, appears early 20s. Toned spear maiden. Azure and white high-cut
  battle leotard with gold trim, bare shoulders and back, armored gauntlets and boots, a short
  half-cape. High honey-blonde ponytail, confident grin. Long golden spear with a sun blade.
  Swept-back wings, thin halo.
- **Nuriel**, Rare Assassin, adult man, mid-20s. Lean and muscular. Sleeveless hooded sky-blue
  scout coat worn open over a bare chest, low-slung belts, wrapped forearms, white cloth mask
  over nose and mouth. Messy pale-gold hair. Twin glowing light daggers. Small sharp wings,
  faint halo.

## Infernal (crimson, ember orange, obsidian, gold)

- **Mordessa**, Legendary Striker, appears around 30. Voluptuous, regal demon queen. Huge
  curled black ram horns with a small crown between them. Long wavy black hair with crimson ends.
  Black-and-crimson armored dress: corseted bodice with a deep neckline, gold filigree, one
  armored shoulder, high-slit skirt, garters and thigh-high boots. Greatsword wreathed in flame
  on her shoulder. Leathery bat wings, spaded tail. Commanding smirk.
- **Brakka**, Epic Guardian, adult man. Enormous, heavily muscled red-skinned demon.
  Shirtless with a chain harness, obsidian armor kilt with glowing magma cracks, huge molten
  gauntlets, short thick horns, tusked friendly grin, thick tail.
- **Vesper**, Epic Caster, appears late 20s. Sultry succubus scholar. Long straight wine-red hair,
  half-lidded eyes, small curved horns. Black and purple: high-collared open-front cape over a
  plunging bodysuit with side cutouts, thigh-high stockings with garters. Floating hellfire orb.
  Bat wings, heart-spaded tail.
- **Imra**, Rare Support, **adult woman**, appears early 20s, petite but with clearly adult
  proportions and face. Playful imp shaman: crimson patchwork wrap top and layered loincloth
  skirt, bone and gold jewelry, short spiky orange hair, small horns, pointed ears, toothy grin.
  Lantern staff with a green-orange flame. Small bat wings, curly tail.
- **Kessa**, Rare Assassin, appears mid-20s. Athletic, curvy hellcat bounty hunter. Sleek black
  bodysuit with strategic cutouts and red straps, long red scarf, black cat ears plus small horns,
  short choppy black hair with a red streak, amber slit-pupil eyes, fang smile. Twin curved
  sickles. Long cat tail.

## Fallen (violet, silver, teal-ash, black)

- **Noctaire**, Legendary Assassin, appears late 20s. Tall, elegant fallen executioner. Long
  straight silver hair, cold violet eyes, cracked dark halo tilted on her head. Tattered black
  gothic dress: corset, open back, black lace sleeves, very high slit, silver buckles and
  chains. Tattered black feathered wings with violet tips. Huge crescent scythe with a violet glow.
  Melancholic allure.
- **Orrin**, Epic Guardian, adult man, around 30. Broad, scarred fallen knight. Cracked silver
  armor broken away to expose a muscular chest and arm, wrapped in broken chains, violet cape.
  Shaggy ash-grey hair, scar over one eye. Chain-wrapped tower shield. Ragged grey wings, broken
  halo hanging from his belt.
- **Sable**, Epic Support, appears late 20s. A fallen nun reimagined: black and violet veil,
  long dark-teal hair, a fitted dark habit with corset lacing, high slits and side panels over
  thigh-high stockings, silver trim. Silver censer trailing violet smoke. Grey wings, dim halo.
  Gentle, sad, alluring.
- **Lucen**, Rare Striker, adult man, mid-20s. Lean, flamboyant duelist. Violet-and-silver
  coat worn open over a bare chest, frilled collar, fitted trousers and tall boots, slim rapier.
  Wavy white hair with black tips, flirtatious smile. One white wing, one black wing. No halo.
- **Ashra**, Rare Caster, appears mid-20s. Sleepy, sultry ash witch. Oversized grey witch hat
  with silver star pins, off-shoulder layered violet robes with a cropped top and a long slit
  skirt with a burned hem. Messy grey hair with violet streaks, cracked violet halo. Crooked wand
  with violet flame. Small grey wings.
