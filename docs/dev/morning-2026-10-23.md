# Morning summary, 2026-10-23

What changed overnight (2026-10-22, 12:00-14:00 UTC; 22:00-midnight on the owner's clock), all
committed to `claude/game-planning` and built. The last IPA has everything below.

## New things to try on the phone

- **Hold your thumb on the Lobby heroine.** After a moment the phone beats with her heart (a soft
  lub-dub through the vibration) and a small printed heart-monitor strip appears beside your thumb.
  The count climbs the longer you hold; her face follows; hold long and she says something when you
  let go. Tell me if the vibration is too strong or too faint.
- **Real days.** The game knows the calendar: New Year, Valentine's, Halloween (the first one, on
  the 31st), Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve, and a birthday for each of the 40 heroines.
  On the day, her Lobby line is about it and "HALLOWEEN · STAMP IT" waits by the date: tap it and a
  red rubber stamp lands on the date, then a gift (once a year). Her roster card wears a BIRTHDAY tag.
- **Your birthday.** The intake form (Settings > Replay the opening) now asks 02 BORN, day and
  month, optional. On that day she says "Happy birthday, <your name>" and the date waits for its
  stamp (300 gems and a scroll).
- **She notices.** Besides long absences: a time zone change ("Your clock jumped 3 hours. Where did
  you take me?"), opening the game between 1 and 4 a.m., an early start, a tenth visit in a day.
  Each once.
- **The War map** is a 3D relief of the battlefield (from your terrain-slab reference): Heaven's
  cliffs at the back, Hell's cracked basin at the front, each sector's front line painted live,
  lava in the cracks, clouds over Heaven. It catches the light as you tilt the phone.
- **The District** is an architect's model: eight buildings with their own architecture (white clay
  for Heaven, black obsidian for Hell), unbuilt ones as frosted ghosts. Buildings now **level up
  with gold** (1 to 5, up to triple the idle bonus): tap a building in the ledger.
- **Shop:** tap a skin's small print (VIEW) and she opens full screen in that look, with BUY.
- **Profile:** each commendation stamp carries the date you earned it, like a postmark.
- **The Lobby poster ages** with your account: a fold after a month, sun-warmed edges after 100
  days, pin holes after a year. The game never mentions it.
