# Vision

Status: **Draft v0.1**. Everything here is a proposal to argue about, not a decision.

## Elevator pitch (draft)

> Heaven and Hell's war is over. Its survivors are not. Gather angels, demons and the fallen
> caught between them into one team, earn rewards while you're away, and decide what happens
> to the realms they left behind.

An idle/AFK gacha RPG for phones: short daily sessions, collectible characters with real
personalities, and team-building choices that actually change the outcome of battles.

## Why players would pick us over Idle Angels

Idle Angels is a long-running but mid-tier game, and the angel-gacha niche is crowded (see
`reference-idle-angels.md`). **The angel/demon theme alone won't make anyone switch.** Its
players' most common complaints point to the opportunities:

| What Idle Angels players complain about | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Pay-to-win; events tuned for big spenders | A clear power ceiling free players can reach; event rewards that a free player can finish |
| Paid outfits grant stats | **Cosmetics are cosmetic.** Skins never add power |
| Mindless "press auto, press skip" combat | Counter-picking and formation matter; optional manual ultimate timing for players who want it |
| Crashes, rollbacks, lost accounts | Server-authoritative saves and account linking from day one; stability before features |
| Slow free progression; opaque odds | Published rates and pity, a wishlist, and level sharing so new heroes are usable right away |
| "Fan service is the only draw" | Characters with story, voice and personality; bond stories as a reason to care |

What players **praise** in Idle Angels that we should match: high-quality character art and
skins, generous free gifts and events, and a low daily time commitment.

## Pillars

Every feature should serve at least one pillar. If it serves none, cut it.

1. **Characters worth collecting.** Art, personality and story are the product. Every hero has a
   recognizable silhouette, a voice, and a bond story.
2. **Progress that respects your time.** Meaningful progress in 10–15 minutes a day. Idle rewards
   keep working when you're away. No chores that exist only to fill time.
3. **Choices that matter, and a fair deal.** Team composition, counters and formation decide
   hard fights. Odds and pity are transparent, and free players can reach the top tier.

## Target players (to validate)

- Adults (roughly 18–35) who enjoy anime-style character collecting and play in short sessions.
- Players of AFK Arena, Idle Heroes, Idle Angels and similar games who are tired of their
  monetization or of having nothing to decide.
- **Open:** target markets and content rating. See `open-questions.md`.

## Hook candidates (pick one to lead with in pre-production)

1. **"Choose a side, or don't."** Every hero belongs to Celestial, Infernal or Fallen. These
   form a counter wheel and also a story choice: chapter endings let the player favor a side,
   which unlocks different bond scenes and cosmetic rewards (never power).
2. **"The fair gacha."** Lead marketing with transparency: published odds, guaranteed pity,
   free top-tier heroes, no stat-granting cosmetics.
3. **"Idle, but tactical."** A clear counter wheel, formation positions and optional manual
   ultimates; hard stages are puzzles rather than walls you can only pay through.

These can be combined, but the store page and trailer need *one* headline.

## Non-goals

- Real-time PvP (expensive, and it doesn't fit idle play).
- A 10-tier rarity ladder (Idle Angels' N to MR is confusing; keep ours short and readable).
- Launching with 100+ heroes. Launch with fewer, better-made heroes and add more steadily.
- Chat and complex guild wars at launch.
